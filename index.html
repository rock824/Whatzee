Game Setup

Add up to 8 players. Humans and bots can play together, and bots support Easy, Normal, and Hard strategies.

How to Play

  • Roll five dice up to three times per turn.
  • Tap dice after a roll to hold them.
  • Choose one unused category to score and end the turn.
  • The upper section earns a 35-point bonus at 63 or more.
  • Your first Whatzee scores 50 points.
  • Extra Whatzees after a scored 50-point Whatzee earn a 100-point bonus.
  • After 13 rounds per player, the highest total wins.
Extra Whatzee bonuses use the familiar bonus/Joker-style behavior: the 100-point bonus is awarded only after the Whatzee box already contains 50.
